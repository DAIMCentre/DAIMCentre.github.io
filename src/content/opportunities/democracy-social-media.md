## The project

This study will explore whether social media data can serve as a faster,
real-time alternative to traditional democracy indices for assessing the health
of democracy in India. Using AI tools, particularly open-sourced large language
models like llama3, the project will analyse Reddit posts to extract key
democratic variables such as trust in government, misinformation, political
polarisation, and media exposure. These variables will then be correlated with
established democracy indices (such as V-Dem and Freedom House) to understand
how online political discourse reflects and influences a country's democratic
health. The ultimate goal is to lay the groundwork for a global early warning
system capable of detecting signs of democratic decline in real time.

## What you will need

**Technical skills.** Python programming (for data collection, processing, and
ML model training); experience with machine learning and NLP techniques (text
classification, sentiment analysis); familiarity with large language models and
APIs; basic data preprocessing and handling of unstructured text data; comfort
working in cloud-based computing environments like Google Colab.

**Domain knowledge.** Understanding of, or interest in, political science
concepts, particularly democracy indices and electoral democracy; awareness of
social media as a data source and its limitations; basic knowledge of research
ethics around data privacy and anonymisation (GDPR awareness).
