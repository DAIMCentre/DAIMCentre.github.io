## The project

This project introduces a sophisticated crime prediction framework that
leverages AI method (like Graph Deep Learning, Reinforcement Learning) within a
spatio-temporal big data context. By synthesising historical crime records with
socio-economic and environmental datasets, the study aims to achieve
high-resolution predictive accuracy across granular spatial and temporal
scales. The resulting system is designed for seamless integration into law
enforcement workflows to optimise public safety interventions.

## What you will need

A working knowledge of Python and machine/deep learning frameworks.
