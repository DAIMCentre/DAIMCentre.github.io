## The project

This project explores the development of foundation models capable of learning
transferable representations from complex engineering and scientific data.
Building on recent advances in deep learning, self-supervised learning and
time-series foundation models, the research aims to develop AI methods that can
generalise across different datasets, engineering systems and prediction tasks.

Potential research topics include time-series forecasting, representation
learning, multimodal data integration, transfer learning and physics-informed
modelling. Applications may span energy systems, intelligent infrastructure,
manufacturing and other scientific and engineering domains.

The project offers opportunities to investigate fundamental AI methodologies
while addressing real-world engineering challenges, contributing to the
development of more generalisable, data-efficient and trustworthy foundation
models.

## What you will need

A working knowledge of Python and machine/deep learning frameworks, with an
interest in foundation models and scientific applications. Experience in
time-series analysis, PyTorch or mathematical modelling would be advantageous.
