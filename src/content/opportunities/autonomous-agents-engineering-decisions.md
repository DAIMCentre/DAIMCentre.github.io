## The project

This project investigates the development of autonomous AI agents capable of
reasoning, planning, learning and making decisions in complex engineering
environments. By integrating large language models, reinforcement learning,
optimisation and multi-agent systems, the research aims to advance AI agents
that can interact with engineering models, digital environments and
computational tools.

Potential research topics include reinforcement learning for sequential
decision-making, LLM-based agent architectures, multi-agent coordination,
tool-augmented reasoning and autonomous scientific experimentation.
Applications may include energy management, intelligent infrastructure,
transportation and industrial operations.

The project aims to develop reliable, adaptive and explainable AI agents that
can support engineering analysis, explore alternative strategies and improve
decision-making under uncertainty.

## What you will need

A working knowledge of Python and machine/deep learning frameworks, with an
interest in reinforcement learning, large language models or autonomous agents.
Experience in optimisation, control systems or engineering simulation would be
advantageous.
