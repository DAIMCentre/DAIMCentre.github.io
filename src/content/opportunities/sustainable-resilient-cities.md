## The project

This is a project that uses artificial intelligence to help cities optimise
resources, reduce carbon emissions, and build resilience against
climate-related challenges. The objectives include improving energy efficiency,
predicting flood and heatwave risks, managing traffic and waste, and analysing
carbon emission patterns across urban sectors such as transport, buildings, and
industry to support net-zero targets.

Methods such as machine/deep learning, computer vision, reinforcement learning
and LLM are applied to data from IoT sensors, satellite imagery, smart energy
grids, emission monitoring stations, and socioeconomic records. The potential
implications include reduced greenhouse gas emissions, lower operational costs,
smarter climate-adaptive infrastructure, and more equitable urban planning,
making AI a critical enabler for the transition toward sustainable and
resilient cities.

## What you will need

Python programming, basic machine learning and data science knowledge (e.g.
scikit-learn, TensorFlow/PyTorch), familiarity with geospatial tools (e.g.
QGIS, GeoPandas), understanding of data wrangling and visualisation, and a
foundational understanding of urban systems, environmental science, or climate
change is beneficial.
