## The project

This project proposes the development of a Human Centered AI system to
proactively identify, assess, and mitigate forced labor risks within complex
global supply chains. By integrating Large Language Models with Retrieval
Augmented Generation and Agentic AI workflows, the system will dynamically
analyse fragmented and unstructured data, such as global news, audit reports,
and legal frameworks, to detect hidden signals of exploitation.

To establish a robust evaluation framework, this approach will be benchmarked
against alternative methodologies. The practical efficacy of the solution will
be validated through a targeted case study focusing on one industry sector to
surface localised vulnerabilities. Crucially, the system moves beyond static
risk assessment by functioning as a remediation engine, generating context
aware, ethical, and legally sound intervention strategies.

## What you will need

A strong foundation in Python programming and practical experience with machine
learning and deep learning methodologies. The ideal candidate will have a
proven ability to build and optimise end to end data pipelines and be highly
familiar with standard artificial intelligence frameworks. Crucially, the
candidate must demonstrate a genuine interest in applying this technology for
social good, specifically in the area of combating modern slavery in global
supply chains.
