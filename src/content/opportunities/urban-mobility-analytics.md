## The project

Leveraging AI methodologies is instrumental in extracting actionable insights
for urban planning and public resource management. As optimising mobility is
fundamental to sustainable development and equitable planning, this project
investigates complex urban movement patterns through the analysis of diverse
big data using advanced AI techniques, such as Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and
Large Language Models (LLMs). Examples of research focus include identifying
the socio-spatial drivers of commuting behaviour, forecasting
origin-destination (OD) flows, and utilising current big data to conduct demand
analysis for urban planning.

## What you will need

A solid foundation in Python programming and spatial data analysis. Beyond
that, a strong intellectual curiosity and a drive to learn advanced AI methods
to uncover deeper insights within complex urban datasets.
