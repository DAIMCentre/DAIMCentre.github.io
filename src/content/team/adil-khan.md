Professor of Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence, with more than
sixteen years of research, development and teaching across both traditional
machine learning and deep learning.

He has built industrial solutions for action and expression recognition, remote
sensing, medical image analysis, natural language processing, and crime and
accident detection. His theoretical work asks how models can be trained without
large amounts of data, generalise to new domains, resist adversarial attacks,
make fair decisions, and avoid catastrophic forgetting.
