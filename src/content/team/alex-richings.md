I am an astrophysicist, with research interests in galaxy formation,
astrochemistry, supermassive black holes, and the interstellar medium. My
research uses numerical simulations to study the formation and evolution of
galaxies, particularly focussing on the astrochemical modelling of ions and
molecules in these simulations.
