An observational astronomer and applied data scientist who has worked in
research-intensive institutions across two continents. His research covers
extra-galactic astronomy, the large-scale structure of the Universe and
cosmology, together with allied and applied sciences and a current focus on
machine learning and artificial intelligence.

He was DAIM's inaugural Director and now heads the School of Digital and
Physical Sciences, which covers Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics and
DAIM.
