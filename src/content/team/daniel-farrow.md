An astronomer by training, with an M.Phys and a PhD from Durham University and
around eight years in Germany as a postdoctoral researcher working on
cosmology, galaxy surveys and the large-scale structure of the Universe.

He has authored or co-authored over 50 publications in astrophysics and
supervises two astronomy PhD students. More recently he co-authored his first
paper in data science and healthcare.
